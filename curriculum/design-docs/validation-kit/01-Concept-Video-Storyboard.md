# 01 — Concept Video Storyboard (45-60 seconds)

**Goal:** answer "what is Founder Mode?" in under 60 seconds for two audiences: a Roblox-aged kid scrolling TikTok, and an SA adult who could be a Patreon Founding Player.

**Output:** one MP4. Vertical 9:16 (TikTok-native). 45-60s. Soft amapiano backing track. SA-coded text overlays.

**Tools you can build it in (pick one — don't overthink):**
- **CapCut** (free, mobile, fastest) — drag in still frames + text + audio
- **Canva** (free tier, browser, drag-and-drop video templates)
- **Figma → Screen recording → CapCut** (most polished if you already mock in Figma)

**You do NOT need Roblox Studio for this video.** The video is a *concept pitch*, not gameplay footage. Stylized mockups + text overlays > rough Roblox prototype footage at this stage.

---

## Visual style (set this once at the top of CapCut)

- Background: warm dusk-orange (Soweto golden hour) → fade to navy night
- Font: bold sans (CapCut "Inter Bold" or Canva "Montserrat Black"), white text with thin black outline
- Music: amapiano log-drum loop, royalty-free or AI-generated. Sources: Mubert, MusicHero.ai (search "amapiano"), Pixabay → search "amapiano instrumental"
- Pace: 4-second avg per frame. Snappy. No frame longer than 6s.
- Sound effect: single "log drum" hit on key transitions

## Frame-by-frame storyboard

### Frame 1 (0:00 - 0:03) — HOOK
- **Visual:** Black screen. Big white text drops in: **"What if Roblox had a real Mzansi?"**
- **Audio:** Amapiano log drum drops at 0:01
- **Text overlay:** none other than the hook
- **Rationale:** the question is the entire video in one beat. If a kid scrolls past in 3 seconds, this is what they read.

### Frame 2 (0:03 - 0:08) — CULTURAL ESTABLISH
- **Visual:** Stylized Soweto street at golden hour. Tarred main road, dirt side roads, a Quantum minibus driving past in silhouette. Could be a Canva illustration, a Midjourney generation, or a hand-drawn sketch — does NOT need to be Roblox-rendered.
- **Text overlay:** small caption bottom-left: *"Soweto. 4pm."*
- **Audio cue:** distant hadeda call layered under the amapiano

### Frame 3 (0:08 - 0:13) — THE PLAYER PLOT
- **Visual:** Same street, zoom into a single empty plot between two existing buildings. A "FOR SALE" sign in the foreground that says "R500."
- **Text overlay:** **"You start with R500."**
- **Subtitle smaller:** *"And a dream."*

### Frame 4 (0:13 - 0:19) — BUILD MOMENT
- **Visual:** Rough mockup of a spaza shop being assembled — three frames stacked: empty shelf → stocked shelf with bread, cooldrink, airtime → SPAZA sign goes up. Stop-motion feel.
- **Text overlay:** **"Build your spaza."**

### Frame 5 (0:19 - 0:24) — CUSTOMER ARRIVAL
- **Visual:** Side view of the spaza. An NPC in school uniform walks up, "want bubble" appears over her head with a question mark. Mom Test framing implicit.
- **Text overlay:** **"Talk to your customers."**
- **Subtitle smaller:** *"They'll tell you what they actually want."*

### Frame 6 (0:24 - 0:30) — THE LESSON IS THE GAME
- **Visual:** Day Report panel sliding in from the right. Stylized chalkboard with three lines: "Sold today: bread, cooldrink." / "Asked for, not stocked: amapiano data bundles." / "Tomorrow: stock data, cut bread by 1." Text appears as if being written.
- **Text overlay:** **"Read the day. Adjust. Run it back."**

### Frame 7 (0:30 - 0:36) — STOKVEL MOMENT (cultural authenticity)
- **Visual:** Wide shot — three spaza owners (player + 2 NPCs) sitting on plastic chairs, a calendar on a wall behind them with names + months marked. NOT a wheel-of-fortune visual — explicitly a calendar.
- **Text overlay:** **"Stokvel up. Take turns. Build big."**
- **Compliance note:** the calendar visual is your stokvel = savings club proof. Keep this frame in the video; you'll cite it in the Roblox policy email.

### Frame 8 (0:36 - 0:42) — AMBITION FRAME
- **Visual:** Pull back, reveal the player has expanded — second spaza, then a salon, then a taxi rank. Each pop into existence with a soft drum hit.
- **Text overlay:** **"Spaza → Salon → Taxi rank → Empire."**

### Frame 9 (0:42 - 0:48) — IDENTITY FRAME
- **Visual:** Player avatar in kasi streetwear (oversized graphic tee, retro cap, kasi-coded fit), arms folded, neighborhood lit in night-time. Behind them, the empire glows.
- **Text overlay:** **"Founder Mode."**
- **Subtitle smaller:** *"Mzansi v1. Coming to Roblox."*

### Frame 10 (0:48 - 0:55) — CTA
- **Visual:** Roblox icon + "Favorite the page" button mockup. Below it, the actual placeholder Roblox URL (you'll add this AFTER step 04 publishes the page).
- **Text overlay:** **"Favorite to play first."**
- **Subtitle:** the Roblox link

### Frame 11 (0:55 - 0:60) — TAG
- **Visual:** Founder Mode wordmark center. Small SA flag in corner.
- **Text overlay:** **"Built in Mzansi. For everywhere."**

## Production checklist

- [ ] Generate or commission 11 still images (Midjourney, Canva, hand-sketch — all fine)
- [ ] Source one amapiano loop from Pixabay / MusicHero.ai / Mubert (royalty-free, document the license)
- [ ] Record 2-3 soft "log drum" hits as transition SFX (or use CapCut's library)
- [ ] Assemble in CapCut with frame timings above
- [ ] Add white-with-black-outline text overlays per frame
- [ ] Export 9:16 vertical, 1080x1920, MP4
- [ ] Save TWO versions: 60s for TikTok, 30s cut (frames 1, 4, 5, 6, 9, 10) for kid-interview videos

## Pitfalls to avoid

- **Don't make it look like a finished Roblox game.** It will fail the credibility test — kids know Roblox visuals. Lean into the *concept-art* aesthetic; people understand "this is a pitch, not a beta."
- **Don't oversell.** No "the next Brookhaven," no "$10M empire." The video is a question, not a claim.
- **Don't use real brand logos** (Coca-Cola, Vodacom, FNB) in the video. Generic placeholders only — saves you a takedown.
- **Don't make the stokvel look like a wheel or a slot.** Calendar only. This frame is your evidence in the Roblox policy email.
- **Don't auto-translate.** v1 is en-ZA. SA slang in the captions ("phanda," "hustle," "kasi") reads authentic to SA viewers; non-SA viewers will Google or DM. That's good — it builds the niche moat.
