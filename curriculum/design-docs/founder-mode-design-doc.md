## Why this supersedes the prior design

The prior design locked FiveM Tebex script vendoring with "Mzansi Spinning Pack" as v1. A market gap audit conducted afterward (companion doc) surfaced findings that invalidated key premises:

- The "no SA spinning Roblox game" assumption was false. **Mzansi Style** by F1n1ty.za already runs SA spinning culture on Roblox (game ID 126588606601144), going viral on TikTok with #SAMA28 #spincar #e30 #bmw. The Roblox-spinning niche is occupied.
- Multiple mature drift scripts exist on Tebex (RAHE, KJ FIVEM, END SCRIPTS). The original "build a spinning script" framing was redundant — the gap is the SA cultural overlay, not the drift physics.
- The cultural term is **Kasi Flava**, not "Diski Streets." Using the wrong term marks the brand outsider-coded.
- **GeoPoll 2024:** 44% of African gamers say games lack characters/environments like their lives. **Semafor:** African gaming hit $1.8B in 2024. Demand is validated by survey data, not just intuition.
- Tycoon is the highest-monetization Roblox genre and is **culturally empty** for African economies. Township informal economy ($30B+ economic reality, "kasi economy") has zero comprehensive game expression.

The pivot: **Founder Mode on Roblox** as v1. Same product line vision (SA cultural authenticity + creator income + stealth education), better wedge.

## Problem Statement

The South African and broader African informal economy ("kasi economy") is the daily lived experience of tens of millions of people: spaza shops, stokvels, taxi ranks, hawker stands, hair salons, scrapyards. It is a $30B+ economic reality and a cultural identity. It has zero comprehensive game expression on Roblox, the platform where SA youth already spend hundreds of millions of player-hours per month. Western mythology and American suburban tycoons (Adopt Me, Bloxburg) earn millions; African economic-life tycoons earn zero. Founder Mode fills this gap with a free-to-play Roblox tycoon that lets players run a township spaza shop, save into a stokvel, expand to taxi-rank franchises and salons, and stealth-learn entrepreneurship and financial literacy as the core game mechanic.

## Demand Evidence

- **Hard survey data.** GeoPoll 2024 (Africa-wide gaming): 44% of African gamers say there aren't enough games with characters/environments like their lives. Semafor (Feb 2025): African gaming revenues hit $1.8B in 2024. (Companion audit, Pass 2.)
- **Adjacent supply signal.** Brookhaven (largest Roblox game globally) plays Amapiano in updates. Roblox is bringing SA culture in at the platform-content level — a tailwind for SA-native originals.
- **Adjacent product success.** Vetkoek Studios shipped Taxi Ranked (mobile) — four SA university grads bootstrapping. Kasi Lifestyle 3D ships. GTA Mzansi APK is at v4.0.8 with the Kasi Vibes variant. Mzansi Style on Roblox is going viral on TikTok. The audience for SA cultural gaming is provably real and provably willing to engage.
- **Roblox tycoon genre validation.** Adopt Me!, Bloxburg, Lumber Tycoon, Theme Park Tycoon, Restaurant Tycoon — multi-million DAU games, top-100 ARPDAU on the platform. The mechanic is solved. The cultural skin is the differentiator.
- **N=1 origin.** Builder is the user — the spaza shop, the stokvel, the kasi taxi rank are all lived experience for the user. Same authentic-origin pattern that produced Brookhaven (Wonder Works built it for her own friend group), Minecraft (Markus built it for himself), and Adopt Me (DreamCraft built around adoption fantasy).
- **N=3+ specific people in mind** (claimed in office hours, week-1 assignment is to validate).

## Status Quo

What SA youth do today instead:
- Play Adopt Me (suburban-Western fantasy of adopting pets / running a household), Bloxburg (suburban American family-life sim), Brookhaven (American small-town RP). The biggest tycoons and life-sims on Roblox are explicitly Western suburban.
- Watch SA TikTok content about kasi life (spaza shop owner POVs, stokvel explainers, taxi rank vlogs) — passive consumption, no interactive expression.
- Play Mzansi Style on Roblox for SA car culture — but no equivalent for SA economic culture.
- Consume SA-themed mobile games (Taxi Ranked, Kasi Lifestyle 3D, GTA Mzansi APK) — but Roblox audience and mobile-game audience overlap less than expected; Roblox is its own ecosystem.

The cost of this status quo is twofold: **cultural absence** (SA kids assemble their economic mental models from American tycoons) and **economic illiteracy** (SA's youth financial literacy gap is documented; nobody has built the gameified bridge).

## Target User & Narrowest Wedge

**Primary user:** SA / Pan-African Roblox players age 9-18, predominantly mobile (phone) but also PC. Already plays tycoons (Adopt Me, Bloxburg). Recognizes spaza shop / stokvel / taxi rank from their own life. Spends 30-60 min per session, returns 3-5x/week.

**Secondary user (parents/educators):** SA parents who already pay for Roblox Premium for their kids and look for "games my kid can learn from." Kasi-themed financial literacy is a justification.

**Narrowest wedge:** A **single Roblox game — "Founder Mode"** — that ships in 90-120 days with one township map, one spaza-shop tycoon loop, one stokvel mechanic, and one Game Pass. Free-to-play. Robux monetization via Game Pass + Developer Products + Premium Payouts.

**Why this wedge first:**
- Roblox = zero ops burden (matches user's hard constraint). Roblox runs servers, billing, moderation tooling.
- Tycoon = highest-monetization Roblox genre. Mechanic is well-understood; Lua patterns are documented; Claude can write the entire codebase.
- Cultural fit is unambiguous and the niche is empty. No competitor has shipped this.
- Mission-credible without being "an educational game" (which kids smell instantly). Stealth-education through play.
- Pan-African TAM: the spaza/buka/duka format generalizes across the continent. v2-v3 can localize maps to Lagos / Nairobi / Accra without rebuilding the loop.

## Constraints

- **Solo developer + Claude.** No co-founder. No engineering team. Claude writes Lua, builds Roblox Studio data structures, designs DataStores, handles RemoteEvents, builds Game Pass purchase flows.
- **Hard no-ops constraint.** Roblox satisfies this natively — Roblox runs the servers, billing, matchmaking, moderation tools. User publishes the game; Roblox handles operations.
- **Time horizon: v1 in 90-120 days.** This is longer than the prior FiveM-script estimate because a polished tycoon is bigger than a script bundle. Anything not in v1 scope is cut.
- **Art pipeline unknown.** User's 3D modeling / Roblox Studio art skill is not yet established. Likely needs: a mix of Roblox UGC marketplace assets (cheap, $1-50 per asset), Roblox Studio's built-in primitives, and possibly one freelance 3D artist for hero assets (spaza shop signage, the gusheshe taxi NPC vehicle, township arch). **Open question — see below.**
- **DevEx requires Roblox Premium and US tax forms (or W-8BEN-E for non-US).** SA-resident DevEx onboarding is doable but requires international banking setup. This is a week-1 logistical assignment.
- **Roblox content moderation.** Founder Mode content must comply with Roblox community standards. Specifically: no representation of selling alcohol/cigarettes (real spaza shops do; the game cannot). No depiction of cash-in-transit heists or violent crime. Stokvel mechanic must not be presented as gambling. **These constraints are real and must be designed around from day 1.**

## Premises

User must agree before v1 build begins. Original premises (from prior design) that still hold:

1. **The educational/STEM mission is real and important but cannot be the wedge.** Founder Mode ships entrepreneurship/financial-literacy stealth-education AS the core mechanic — but it is marketed as a tycoon game, not as edutainment. Roblox kids smell "education" and bounce.
2. **Solo dev + Claude can ship one Roblox tycoon in 90-120 days only if v1 has exactly one map, one core loop, one Game Pass, and one Developer Product. No multiple maps, no multiple businesses in v1, no character customization beyond minimal.**

New premises driven by audit findings:

3. **Roblox is the right platform for SA youth who play games.** SA Roblox audience is large, mobile-friendly, and culturally permeable (Brookhaven amapiano updates prove this). FiveM is now a v3+ candidate (or skipped entirely).
4. **Tycoon is a solved Roblox genre at the mechanic level.** The differentiator is cultural fit, not gameplay innovation. Don't try to invent a new genre on top of cultural authenticity.
5. **Roblox content moderation rules constrain the design.** Real spaza shops sell beer and cigarettes; Founder Mode cannot. Real stokvels are technically informal banking; Founder Mode's stokvel mechanic must read as "savings club," never "gambling." We design with these guardrails from day 1, not retrofit later.
6. **Pan-African TAM is real but v2.** v1 ships SA-localized (township aesthetic, isiZulu/isiXhosa NPC names and signage). v2 adds Lagos, Nairobi, Accra maps once SA proves out.

7. **Every game mechanic must double as a stealth-pedagogy carrier for entrepreneurial concepts** (lean startup, design thinking, business model canvas, customer discovery, build-measure-learn, unit economics, pivot-vs-persevere, capital pooling). If a mechanic doesn't carry a concept, it doesn't ship in v1. If a concept can only be taught via tutorial copy or a quiz screen, it gets cut. The stealth-pedagogy layer is non-negotiable and is the moat that separates Founder Mode from a generic skin-on-Bloxburg.

## Approaches Considered (recap from audit + new options)

### Approach A: Refined original — SA Asset Pack on Tebex (rejected)
Repositioned spinning pack as asset overlay on existing FiveM drift scripts. Rejected because: keeps user inside FiveM B2B (RP server owners, ~10-50 globally) when Roblox B2C is 100x larger TAM and matches no-ops constraint better.

### Approach B: Pan-African RP Framework on Tebex (deferred)
Cross-African FiveM RP framework. Better TAM than SA-only spinning. Rejected for v1 because: still requires the user to wade into the FiveM RP server-owner ecosystem, where Made in Lagos has years of head-start. Could revisit as a later product line.

### Approach C: African Mythology RPG on Roblox (deferred)
Anansi/Tokoloshe/Sangoma. Globally empty niche. Rejected for v1 because: RPG/combat mechanics are *harder* to ship solo than a tycoon. Cultural-narrative depth requires writers, voice acting, lore design. v3-v4 candidate at earliest.

### Approach D: Founder Mode on Roblox (CHOSEN)
Township informal economy simulator. Highest-monetization Roblox genre. Empty cultural niche. Mission-aligned via stealth-education. No-ops via Roblox-native publishing. Solo + Claude tractable. Pan-African scaling path.

## Recommended Approach (locked)

**Approach D — Founder Mode on Roblox.** v1 ships in 90-120 days. Sequenced product roadmap follows.

### v1 Product Spec — "Founder Mode"

**Core loop**
- Player spawns in a township map (Soweto-styled: tarred main road, dirt side roads, taxi rank in distance, hadeda calls in ambient audio).
- Tutorial NPC (a "gogo" / grandmother character) gifts player an empty plot and starter capital (R500 in-game).
- Player builds a basic spaza shop structure (place a wall, place a counter, place a fridge) using a build-mode UI similar to Bloxburg's.
- Stock products at a wholesale supplier across town (drive a bicycle / quantum taxi to fetch). Initial stock: bread, milk, chips, cooldrink, airtime vouchers, electricity prepaid vouchers.
- NPC customers walk in throughout the day-night cycle. Some pay cash, some pay on credit ("slate"). Player chooses who to extend credit to.
- Earn revenue. Reinvest in: more shelves, more product variety, better signage, paying staff, expanding to a salon next door, or adding a taxi-rank franchise.

**Stokvel mechanic (the cultural-financial differentiator)**
- Player can join or start a stokvel with up to 10 other real players (or NPCs in single-player).
- Each member contributes a fixed amount per "month" (game time = 20 min real-time = 1 month game-time).
- Each month one member receives the full pot, rotation order set at start.
- Use cases: pre-purchase commercial fridge for spaza, buy a Quantum minibus for taxi franchise, pay staff in advance.
- **Roblox content moderation guardrail:** stokvel is presented as a savings club rotation, NEVER as gambling. No randomness in payout order. Pre-set rotation only.

**Cultural touches (the cultural-authenticity differentiator)**
- Load shedding events (lights out for 2 game-hours, fridge contents spoil if no inverter). Player can buy an inverter Game Pass.
- Township music genres rotating in the background (amapiano in afternoon, gospel on Sunday morning, kwaito in evening). All royalty-free / AI-generated stems.
- NPC dialogue in mixed English / isiZulu / isiXhosa / Sesotho. "Sawubona, I'll take a loaf of bread, ngi-cela the airtime futhi."
- Hadeda birds, taxi hooters, and church-bell ambient audio.
- Real product references where Roblox moderation allows (Coca-Cola is too risky; "Cool Drink Co." brand is fine). Bread brands are generic. Airtime / data brands are generic ("Fone Mobile," "DataLink").

**Monetization**
- **Game Pass: "VIP Spaza" (199 Robux ~ $2.49)** — exclusive products in stock, faster restocking, premium signage, an inverter that makes load shedding profitable instead of catastrophic, exclusive outfit (kasi streetwear).
- **Developer Product: "Kasi Cash" packs** (50 / 200 / 500 Robux for in-game R-currency boosts) — convertible to in-game capital for faster expansion.
- **Premium Payouts** — Roblox Premium subscribers playing the game generate passive Robux for the developer per minute.
- **Cosmetic Game Passes (post-launch):** outfit packs, premium spaza decor sets, custom signage.

**Persistence**
- DataStore for player progress: capital, spaza inventory, stokvel membership, expansion state, owned cosmetics.
- DataStoreService with proper retry / error handling. Claude writes the boilerplate.

#### Stealth Pedagogy Layer (the entrepreneurial education spine)

The game teaches startup thinking without ever using the word "lesson," "course," "quiz," or "education." Every mechanic maps to an underlying concept from design thinking, lean startup, business model generation, and the startup owner's manual. Players learn by *doing* — the mechanics ARE the curriculum.

**Design rule:** if a player can complete an action only by reading instructions, the mechanic has failed the stealth-education bar. Concepts surface through consequence, not through copy.

| Underlying concept (real-world) | In-game mechanic (player experience) | Learning fires when... |
|---|---|---|
| **Customer discovery** (Steve Blank, Four Steps to the Epiphany) | NPC customers walk in with visible "wants" bubble. Player can either guess what to stock OR talk to NPCs first ("eyya, what you looking for sisi?") and hear their actual need. Players who talk-first stock the right products and earn 3x faster. | Player who guess-stocks fails. Player who talks-first wins. The lesson "talk to customers before you build" is felt, not taught. |
| **Problem-Solution Fit** (Lean Canvas, Ash Maurya) | Each township district has different unmet needs (student dorm = airtime/instant noodles; taxi rank = water/Grand-Pa headache powders; church row = Sunday-best snacks). Stocking generic doesn't work. | Player who copies a successful spaza into a different district and fails learns that solutions are context-specific. |
| **Minimum Viable Product (MVP)** | Starter capital is intentionally tiny (R500). You CANNOT build a full shop. You must pick 3 products, one shelf, no signage. Make money from that. THEN expand. | Player who tries to build big up-front runs out of money before opening. Player who ships small, sells, reinvests, expands wins. The MVP lesson is enforced by economy, not explained. |
| **Build-Measure-Learn loop** (Eric Ries, The Lean Startup) | A "Day Report" appears at end of each in-game day: what sold, what didn't, what customers asked for that you didn't have. No grading. No "you should." Just data. | Player iterates stock based on the report. The loop becomes second-nature within 3 in-game days. |
| **Pivot vs. persevere** | When sales flatline for 3 in-game days, NPC customers start saying things that hint at a pivot ("akusekho la abantu, the salon down the road is busier" / "everyone wants Wi-Fi vouchers nowadays"). Player can ignore (persevere) or pivot. Both are valid; both have consequences. | The concept of pivot is felt as a creative choice, not learned as a vocab word. |
| **Business Model Canvas** (Osterwalder) | At Game Pass tier, the player unlocks a "Spaza Plan" UI — visually styled as a chalkboard divided into 9 zones (customers, channels, value prop, revenue, costs, key activities, key resources, partners, customer relationships). Each zone fills in based on what the player has built. | Players see their own business mapped without ever seeing the words "Business Model Canvas." When they later encounter the canvas in school or YC content, they recognize it. |
| **Unit economics** (CAC, LTV, gross margin) | Every product shows wholesale cost vs. retail price as you stock it. Repeat customers (visible loyalty meter above NPC heads) buy more over time. Marketing actions (paint your shop wall, sponsor the local soccer team) cost money and bring more customers. | Player who marks up too high loses repeat customers. Player who undermarks bleeds margin. Player who over-spends on marketing without retention dies. The CAC/LTV/margin trinity is felt, not formula'd. |
| **Stokvel = informal venture syndicate / capital pooling** | The stokvel mechanic is functionally a pre-seed syndicate. 10 members pool monthly; each takes a turn. Used for big purchases that no member could fund alone (commercial fridge, Quantum minibus). | Players experience capital pooling, rotating credit, and trust-based finance — concepts central to bootstrapped venture building, framed in their grandma's stokvel language. |
| **Cash flow vs. profit** | Day Report shows "money in pocket today" vs. "money owed (slate)" separately. Players who extend too much credit go broke despite "selling well." | The "profitable on paper, broke in practice" lesson is taught the only way it's ever truly learned: by going broke. |
| **Critical thinking under uncertainty** (Black Swans, second-order effects) | Random events: load shedding, taxi strike (no customers), spaza next door drops prices, gogo's funeral (everyone needs catering). No predictability. | Players develop pattern recognition and contingency thinking. Insurance Game Pass becomes a real-options decision. |
| **Design thinking — empathy, ideate, prototype, test** (IDEO / d.school) | "New Product" mode: player can propose a new product to stock that isn't on the wholesaler's list (custom-named, custom-priced). NPCs react to it: ignore, scoff, buy once, buy repeatedly. Player tests by shipping a small batch. | Players run their own design-thinking loop on every new product idea. Empathy = NPC dialogue. Ideate = product naming. Prototype = small batch. Test = NPC reaction. |
| **Venture building / multi-bet portfolio** (Disciplined Entrepreneurship, Aulet) | Late-game: player can spawn a SECOND business in a different district (salon, taxi franchise, scrapyard). Each is its own P&L; some succeed, some die. Stokvel acts as cross-business capital allocator. | Players experience portfolio thinking — diversification, dead-bet acceptance, capital reallocation. Same skills a fund manager or serial founder uses. |
| **Real-world learning through building** | The entire game IS this principle. Nothing is taught; everything is built. | Continuous. |

**Why this stealth-pedagogy design works**

- Roblox kids smell "edutainment" in seconds and bounce. The genre is graveyards of well-intentioned games. Founder Mode avoids the bounce by being a *good tycoon first*. The pedagogy is the architecture, not the marketing.
- Real-world reference: Roller Coaster Tycoon teaches operations management, queue theory, and pricing dynamics to anyone who plays it for 40 hours. None of those words appear in the game. Players who go on to study OR or hospitality recognize the concepts. Founder Mode does the same for entrepreneurship.
- The Game Pass-locked Business Model Canvas view is a deliberate design choice: it's the visual bridge between play-knowledge and named-knowledge. Players who pay 199 Robux for VIP get the canvas; when they later read Osterwalder or take a Coursera course, they recognize the chalkboard from their game.
- The stokvel mechanic is the cultural-pedagogy fusion: it teaches venture-syndicate thinking using a structure SA kids ALREADY know from their grandmothers, mothers, aunties. Pan-African scaling is automatic — every African culture has a version (esusu in Nigeria, susu in Ghana, chama in Kenya, ROSCA globally).

**What it does NOT teach (intentionally cut for v1)**

- Equity / cap tables / fundraising mechanics: too abstract for the tycoon-genre reader, defer to v3+ as a "Stokvel Wars" multiplayer mode where players issue equity to investor NPCs.
- Term sheets / legal: too dry, not gameplay-tractable, never ship.
- Engineering / CS / robotics: this was the prior "Ubuntu Robotics" mission slot. Cut from this game; lives in a separate later product if ever.

**Mapping to the canonical entrepreneurship texts (for the brand-partnership pitch deck)**

When pitching FNB / Capitec / Yoco / Game Stores / NGO partners, this is the lineage you can credibly cite:

- **The Lean Startup** (Eric Ries) — Build-Measure-Learn loop is the daily report mechanic.
- **Business Model Generation** (Osterwalder & Pigneur) — Spaza Plan chalkboard is the canvas.
- **The Startup Owner's Manual** (Steve Blank) — Customer discovery is the talk-first-stock-second mechanic.
- **Running Lean / Lean Canvas** (Ash Maurya) — Problem-solution fit is the per-district stocking mechanic.
- **Disciplined Entrepreneurship** (Bill Aulet) — Multi-business late-game is the 24-step framing.
- **The Mom Test** (Rob Fitzpatrick) — NPC dialogue is designed so generic questions ("would you buy this?") produce useless answers; specific questions about past behavior ("when did you last buy bread?") produce useful ones.
- **Zero to One** (Peter Thiel) — Niche-monopoly mechanic: dominating one product in one district beats being mediocre across many.
- **The Hard Thing About Hard Things** (Ben Horowitz) — Random crisis events (load shedding, taxi strike, funeral) train resilience under uncertainty.
- **Ubuntu philosophy + stokvel tradition** (the African contribution to global entrepreneurship literature) — capital pooling, mutual trust, rotating credit. This is the part Western canon missed and Founder Mode centers.

This pedagogy lineage is the credibility backbone for any educator, parent, NGO, or financial-services partner who asks "what is your kid actually learning?" — and the answer is "more than they would in a semester of intro entrepreneurship, with zero awareness they're learning it."

# ENG REVIEW LOCKS (2026-04-27)

The following decisions were locked via /plan-eng-review on 2026-04-27. They override anything earlier in this doc that contradicts. They were challenged by an independent outside-voice review (Claude subagent, Codex unavailable) and most outside-voice findings were accepted.

## Architecture (locked)

- **DataStore wrapper:** ProfileService (loleris). Session locking, retry, schema migration. NOT raw DataStoreService.
- **Server-authoritative economy:** All buy/sell/place/claim actions through RemoteEvents validated server-side. Client renders only.
- **Anti-exploit module:** `ServerScriptService/Systems/AntiExploit.lua`. Validates capital, inventory bounds, district legitimacy, rate-limits per-RemoteEvent. Non-negotiable v1 work (not stretch).
- **NPC computation:** Server-side single instances replicated to clients. No client-side AI. PathfindingService capped at 20 active requests/server (named regulars only); waypoint graph + simple steering for the remaining ~230 NPCs.
- **MarketplaceService ProcessReceipt:** Persist FIRST, then return PurchaseGranted. Idempotent on retry.
- **BindToClose:** Parallel `task.spawn` per ProfileService :Release() to fit 30s window.
- **StreamingEnabled = true.** Mandatory for mobile.
- **Autosave cadence:** 90s + on PlayerRemoving + on BindToClose.
- **Audio budget:** ~25 audio assets × ~150 Robux upload = ~3,750 Robux upfront budget.

## District topology — 1.1B (locked, holds the line)

**Shared multiplayer districts.** Multiple players' spazas in the same district draw from a shared NPC pool. Real player-vs-player competitive pressure. Highest pedagogy fidelity (all 7 entrepreneurship beats: customer discovery, competition, pricing pressure, market saturation, networks, status/signaling, solo-learner ramp). Highest ship risk for a first Roblox project. **Outside voice flagged this as 12-18 months of work; user explicitly chose to hold the line. v1 timeline therefore is ~9 months, not 4-5 months.**

## Day-night cycle — 1.2A (locked)

24 min real = 1 in-game day. ~12 real-hours = 1 in-game month. Compounds learning across player sessions. BML loop fires at the right pace.

## Mobile-first — 1.3A (locked)

Every UI tested on mid-tier Android (target device, see 4.10A) before it ships. Build mode uses gesture controls (drag-place, pinch-rotate). +25-30% UI dev time but captures 100% of SA Roblox audience.

## Save schema + loyalty granularity — 1.4A (locked)

50 named regular NPCs each with a loyalty record + last-purchase memory. ~500 unnamed walk-by NPCs aggregated as district loyalty bucket. Single ProfileStore, ~15-30KB profile, well under 4MB cap. `schema_version=1` field for future migration.

## Code-quality conventions (locked)

- Data-driven config tables in `ReplicatedStorage/Config/` for dialogue, products, events, district weights.
- Module layout: `ServerScriptService/Systems/`, `ReplicatedStorage/Modules/`, `ReplicatedStorage/Remotes/`, `StarterPlayerScripts/UI/`.
- All boundary calls (DataStore, MarketplaceService, PathfindingService) wrapped in `pcall` + 1 retry + graceful degrade. Reusable `Modules/SafeCall.lua`.
- Naming: PascalCase modules/types, camelCase vars/functions, SCREAMING_SNAKE constants.
- No clever infra (no ECS, no DI, no event bus). Standard Roblox-Lua patterns.

## RETENTION SPINE (added 2026-04-27 post-audit)

The eng-review locked the curriculum spine (12 mechanics → entrepreneurship concepts) and the architecture spine (ProfileService, anti-exploit, server-authoritative, 1.1B districts). A subsequent market audit surfaced that the design was missing the **retention spine** — the standard Roblox tycoon mechanics that every successful tycoon ships and that turn a 30-minute first-session into a 6-month habit.

**Reference:** e.l.f. UP! (22.1M lifetime visits, 1.29M monthly, 96% approval) ships every mechanic in this section. So does Restaurant Tycoon 3 (10.5K CCU), Build a Tycoon, Ultimate Mining Tycoon. Without these mechanics, Founder Mode would be the most pedagogically rigorous tycoon ever built that nobody plays past day 7.

**Design rule:** every retention mechanic must ALIGN with the curriculum, not fight it. If a mechanic teaches "log in daily because we say so" instead of "log in daily because the stokvel month rolls over," it gets redesigned. The retention spine is curriculum-compatible by construction.

### Retention mechanics — full v1 spec

#### 1. Prestige / "Graduation" loop (CRITICAL — ships v1)

**In-game:** When the player reaches "Spaza Magnate" tier (capital ≥ R1,000,000 + 5 expansion locations + stokvel completed at least one full rotation), they unlock the option to "graduate" their first kasi empire — sell it to an NPC investor for a permanent multiplier and restart in a new district with a new starter capital boost.

**Curriculum alignment:** This IS the entrepreneurship lesson of "exit and restart." Real founders sell companies and start new ones. The mechanic teaches the venture-portfolio concept directly. The "graduate" framing is curriculum-language, not gamble-language.

**Roblox payoff:** Infinite progression loop. Players who would have left at "I won" now have a reason to come back. Standard tycoon-genre retention amplifier.

**Permanent bonuses earned per graduation:**
- +5% revenue multiplier (stacks across graduations)
- One unlocked cosmetic from a previous district
- Exclusive "Magnate Class" outfit slot
- Veteran-only NPC dialogue lines ("Eyy, Mama Thandi remembers when you started")

**v1 implementation cost:** ~1 week. Mostly UI + reset logic + permanent-state field in ProfileService.

#### 2. Daily login bonus — "Stokvel Week" rotation (ships v1, post-Roblox-policy-clearance)

**In-game:** A 7-day rotation tied to the in-game stokvel meeting cycle. Each day the player logs in:
- Day 1 (Monday): "Stokvel meeting bonus" = R200 + reputation +1
- Day 2 (Tuesday): "Wholesaler discount" = 10% off all stock purchases for 24h
- Day 3 (Wednesday): "Customer-discovery boost" = NPC dialogue reveals 2 extra preference signals
- Day 4 (Thursday): "Cash-flow bonus" = R500 + extra slate-collection chance
- Day 5 (Friday): "Pay-day rush" = +50% customer arrival rate for 1 in-game day
- Day 6 (Saturday): "Funeral-week catering" = snack/drink demand spike (event tie-in)
- Day 7 (Sunday): "Church-row spike" = Sunday-best snack demand AND a permanent cosmetic drop

Miss a day = chain breaks, restart at Day 1. Don't miss a day = Day 7 cosmetic.

**Curriculum alignment:** Each day's bonus is an in-curriculum lesson moment, not an arbitrary "log in for free stuff." Stokvel meetings, wholesaler discounts, customer-discovery boosts, cash-flow vs. profit (slate), demand spikes — every bonus reinforces a real entrepreneurship concept.

**Roblox payoff:** Habit loop. The single highest-leverage retention mechanic in mobile/Roblox. Without it, D7 retention plummets.

**v1 implementation cost:** ~1 week. Daily check + rotation index in ProfileService + 7 small UI states.

#### 3. Seasonal events — SA-coded (3 events in v1, more in v1.1+)

**In-game (v1):**
- **Heritage Day (24 September):** "Heritage Spaza" cosmetic pack drops free for 7 days. NPCs wear traditional outfits. Sell-through rate on traditional snack items spikes 3x. Limited-time signage with isiZulu/isiXhosa proverbs.
- **Festive Season (15 December – 5 January):** "Festive Stokvel" — special rotation pays out double on Boxing Day. Decorations go up. Carols and Afro-house holiday tracks rotate in. Higher customer arrival rates.
- **Easter / Long Weekend:** "Funeral / family gathering" cycle — catering-demand spike events fire 4x more often. Players who stocked correctly profit massively.

**Curriculum alignment:** Real entrepreneurs in SA know the calendar matters. Heritage Day is a real spike, festive season is a real spike, funeral catering is a real economy. The seasonal events ARE the lesson on calendar-driven demand forecasting (a real entrepreneurship skill).

**Roblox payoff:** Limited-time content drives return visits. e.l.f. UP! does this monthly. Founder Mode's SA cultural calendar is a free differentiator nobody else has.

**v1 implementation cost:** ~2 weeks total for 3 events. Each event is a config-table addition (spawn weights, cosmetic drops, NPC dialogue overrides) — not new systems.

#### 4. Limited-time Game Pass drops (ships v1.1)

**In-game:** Standard Game Pass at 199 Robux. But once per month, "VIP Spaza Weekend" runs Friday-Sunday at 99 Robux (50% off). Time-limited, visible countdown timer in-game.

**Curriculum alignment:** Pricing strategy lesson — limited-time discounts work in real spaza shops too. The game IS demonstrating dynamic pricing.

**Roblox payoff:** Urgency = conversion. Standard pattern. Increases lifetime ARPDAU by 15-30% in industry data.

**v1.1 implementation cost:** ~3 days. Time-gated price config + UI countdown.

#### 5. Idle / automation layer (ships v1)

**In-game:** Once player has hired their first staff member, the spaza generates "regular customer" revenue while the player is offline (capped at 8 hours per offline period to prevent overnight farming). When player returns, an "Auto-cashed" panel shows what was sold to known regular customers (Mama Thandi bought her usual; Bra Sipho bought his usual). NEW customers (district-mass NPCs) only arrive during ACTIVE play.

**Curriculum alignment (the critical design): idle revenue ONLY comes from regulars you've already developed via customer discovery.** This rewards the "talk to your customer" mechanic — players with more named regulars earn more idle income. Pure idle without customer discovery would distort the curriculum; this design integrates it.

**Roblox payoff:** "Leaves it running" pattern. Massive retention. The reason kids open the game while doing homework. Tycoon-genre standard.

**v1 implementation cost:** ~1 week. Offline-time delta calculation + regular-customer revenue formula + welcome-back UI panel.

#### 6. Expanded cosmetic ladder (ships v1, expands v1.1+)

**In-game:** Multiple Game Passes (vs. the original doc's single VIP), each with a clear theme:

| Game Pass | Robux | What it unlocks |
|---|---:|---|
| **VIP Spaza** (1 in v1) | 199 | Premium products, faster restock, exclusive signage, inverter, VIP outfit |
| **Kasi Streetwear** (v1.1) | 99 | 8 outfit pieces (graphic tees, retro caps, kasi-coded fits) |
| **Quantum Owner** (v1.1) | 199 | Personal Quantum minibus, 2 paint jobs, taxi-route access |
| **Stokvel Veteran** (v1.2) | 99 | Stokvel-exclusive badges, calendar themes, name-tag colors |

Plus dozens of cosmetic items as Developer Products (one-time purchases, R5-50 in-game = 50-500 Robux).

**Curriculum alignment:** Cosmetics are status-signaling, which is itself an entrepreneurship lesson — visible success matters. Founders dress, founders display, founders signal.

**Roblox payoff:** Cosmetic surface = monetization surface. Adopt Me makes $60M/year on pets. Founder Mode's equivalent: outfits + vehicles + signage + badges. A single Game Pass at 199 Robux caps you at ~$2/converting-player-LTV; a cosmetic ladder pulls $5-15.

**v1 implementation cost:** ~2 weeks for the v1 VIP Spaza + asset uploads. Additional packs ship v1.1+ as content drops.

#### 7. Co-op "Co-Spaza" mode (ships v1.1, NOT v1)

**In-game:** Two players partner to co-own a single spaza. Shared capital pool, shared inventory, split revenue 50/50 (configurable). Either player can stock, sell, talk to customers; both names appear on the signage. Quitting the partnership = clean split of capital.

**Curriculum alignment:** Co-founder dynamics. The classic startup-curriculum lesson on "pick your co-founder carefully" is taught by experiencing the partnership directly.

**Roblox payoff:** Friends-play-together = algorithm boost = home-page placement. Hospital Tycoon 3's success is largely this mechanic.

**v1.1 implementation cost:** ~3 weeks. Shared-state coordination, exit-flow UX, dispute mediation (the partnership "divorce" UI).

**Why v1.1 not v1:** Cross-player shared state on a single plot is the same architectural risk as multiplayer stokvel. Defer until 1.1B shared districts have proven stable.

#### 8. Founder Pass (battle pass) — ships v1

**In-game:** A 30-day track with 30 tiers. Each tier rewards a small in-game item (cosmetic, currency, regular-customer attraction boost). Two parallel tracks:
- **Free track:** all 30 tiers earnable by playing the curriculum normally (sell to customers, hit milestones, complete daily logins). Free track ALONE delivers the full curriculum spine.
- **Premium track (199 Robux):** parallel rewards on the same 30 tiers — exclusive cosmetics, bonus currency, exclusive Quantum vehicle paint job, exclusive stokvel badge. Cosmetic-only.

**Curriculum alignment (CRITICAL):** Free track is the WHOLE curriculum. Paying does NOT teach you more, it just lets you flex more. This is non-negotiable — pay-to-learn would betray the mission. Pay-to-flex is fine.

**Roblox payoff:** 30-day commitment loop. Industry-standard since Fortnite. Drives daily and weekly active.

**v1 implementation cost:** ~2 weeks. Tier track + reward system + UI. Reuses the daily-login chain logic.

**Naming:** "Founder Pass" — explicitly NOT "Battle Pass" (battle is wrong genre). "Founder Pass" reinforces the brand and the mission language.

#### 9. Friend leaderboards (ships v1)

**In-game:** Three leaderboards visible from the Spaza Plan UI:
- **Friends:** top 10 friends' empire valuations (Roblox friend-list integration). Status competition.
- **District:** top 10 spazas in the player's current district (live cohort).
- **Global Mzansi:** top 100 weekly. Unreachable but inspirational.

**Curriculum alignment:** Status competition = real entrepreneurship motivator. Comparing empires teaches the "what does scale look like?" lesson directly.

**Roblox payoff:** Comparison drives return. Friends seeing a friend's empire = "I need to log in and catch up." Standard pattern.

**v1 implementation cost:** ~3 days. OrderedDataStore queries + UI panel.

#### 10. Push notifications (ships v1.1)

**In-game (Roblox app push):** "Your fridge has 1 hour until expiry — load shedding ended." "Mama Thandi is at your spaza waiting for her Tuesday bread." "Your stokvel month rolls over in 30 minutes — claim your payout." Fired sparingly (max 1 per real-day per player) to avoid annoyance.

**Curriculum alignment:** Push notifications mimic real entrepreneur stress signals (your fridge breaks, your loyal customer arrived, your savings club month rolls). The notification IS the lesson on operational vigilance.

**Roblox payoff:** Most underused retention lever on Roblox. Roblox app push opens are a real signal. Notification-opens convert at 12-25% to in-game return-visits.

**v1.1 implementation cost:** ~1 week. Roblox push API integration + per-player frequency cap + opt-in flow.
